About BotCraft

Technology that helps ideas become machines.

BotCraft is a robotics technology brand focused on accessible, dependable hardware for Raspberry Pi-based robotic projects.

We aim to remove the friction from finding practical components so builders can spend more time designing, programming, experimenting, and learning.
